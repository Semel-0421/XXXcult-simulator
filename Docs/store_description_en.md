# XXXcult Simulator — Store Description (EN)

**Build a cult from nothing, bend its faith to your will, and decide whether it becomes a saint's community, a cash machine, or a theocracy — before the world shuts you down.**

XXXcult Simulator is a text-driven life and cult management sim. You live one life from birth: grow up through hundreds of events, collect Gifted traits, and shape a personality pulled between eight Desires and Virtues. Then you found your own cult — and every choice you make as its leader ripples through followers, faith, offerings, authority, doctrine, and reputation.

**What you do**
- **Live a full life.** 200 life events and 394 cult events, each with branching choices that succeed or fail based on your stats.
- **Write your doctrine.** Unlock 120 doctrines across eight branches. Will you preach restraint and charity, or sanctify lust, greed, appetite, and ambition?
- **Survive public opinion.** Reputation moves you from Persecuted to Recognized. Let it hit zero and the state dissolves your cult.
- **Chase dozens of endings.** Final-skill endings, extreme collapses, deaths, and dissolutions — with 100 achievements and a legacy system that makes every new run a little stronger.

**Prepared God mode**
After five endings, play as a god who has descended into a human vessel. Grow a 125-node divine tree across Miracle, Prophecy, Mercy, and Desire, consecrate temples by miracle, and balance the envy of heaven against the suspicion of the mortal world. Unite two cores to ascend — nine ascension endings await.

**Atmosphere**
A dark sacred soundtrack of Gregorian-style chant and pipe organ that shifts with your cult's fate, plus a result screen that shows exactly what each choice cost you.

**Optional adult content (18+)**
Mature events and illustrations appear only after age verification in Options, only when enabled, and only when your character is an adult. They can be turned off at any time.

Every cult starts with one believer. How far will yours go?
